02. Executive Summary

The Graduate Paradox: Tunisia produces over 54,381 university graduates annually [MARKET DATA] (including 3,110 engineers and 6,885 masters), yet higher education unemployment sits at 26.6% (surging to 35.6% for women) and youth unemployment reaches 35.4% (INS Q2 2026). Simultaneously, the tech innovation ecosystem—with 1,165 labeled startups [MARKET DATA] and hundreds of IT service firms (ESNs)—faces severe talent bottlenecks caused by 25–35% annual senior engineer emigration.

The MatchOp Solution: MatchOp (matchop.vercel.app) replaces passive job boards with an algorithmic shortlisting exchange. By evaluating structured student competency vectors against job criteria using 384-dimensional semantic embeddings, MatchOp eliminates the "resume black hole" for candidates and delivers a pre-screened top 5–10 shortlist to employers within 48 to 72 hours, reducing recruiter screening labor by >75%.

Unvarnished Strategic Reality MatchOp is in the pre-launch development phase with 0 registered students and 0 TND in revenue [VERIFIED CURRENT FACT]. However, 3 commercial companies (Solution Creative Events, Nomade Arts, Nexus) have created accounts and expressed collaboration interest. Management is raising an agile 50,000 TND validation tranche to fund legal incorporation, campus club rollout, and prove employer willingness to pay across a 90-day pilot.

06. - 07. The Problem & Structural Market Pain

Candidate Pain (Supply Side)
  • The Resume Black Hole: Students spend 40+ hours/month submitting 50+ applications with a sub-10% response rate.
  • The Experience Paradox: Entry-level roles demanding 2-3 years experience lock out capable graduates whose projects are ignored.
  • Information Asymmetry: No transparent feedback on why applications fail or what technical skills are missing.
Employer Pain (Demand Side)
  • Resume Noise: Sifting through 200–500 unqualified PDFs per job post consumes 15–25 hours of engineering management time.
  • High Senior Turnover: 25–35% annual senior developer attrition to European firms demands continuous junior intake.
  • Prohibitive Legacy ATS Costs: LinkedIn Recruiter ($800+/seat/mo) is economically unfeasible for local currency tech SMEs.

09. - 10. Technology & AI Architecture

MatchOp's technical architecture is built for extreme performance, security, and capital efficiency:

Frontend & Interaction Engine

Core Stack: React 19 SPA bootstrapped with Vite 7.

Mobile Gestures: framer-motion spring physics powering vertical discovery feeds.

Localization: i18next full bilingual English/French engine.

Analytics: Integrated Vercel Speed Insights & Web Vitals tracking.

Backend & AI Services (Supabase)

PostgreSQL 15 + pgvector: 384-dimensional dense vector embeddings for real-time similarity search.

Row Level Security (RLS): Multi-tenant isolation enforced at database level across all tables.

Edge Functions (Deno): swipe-stack, record-swipe, janitor, ai-profile-polisher.

Supabase Realtime: WebSocket messaging channels for instant chat.

External Scraper Subsystem (Python 3.11 Worker)

Housed in scraper/, this standalone worker uses Scrapling to fetch job listings from global and regional ATS boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable). Groq AI extracts structured skills, experience level, and metadata, deduplicating via content_hash and upserting into external_jobs.

15. - 16. Business Model & B2B Pricing Strategy

Strategic Directive: Rejection of Candidate Application Paywalls MatchOp's codebase contains pricing for candidate subscriptions (19 TND/month or 149 TND/year) to bypass swipe caps. With 35.4% youth unemployment and only ~3% mobile wallet penetration in Tunisia, charging candidates creates extreme drop-off and starves platform liquidity. Core candidate discovery must be 100% free. 85% to 90% of revenue will be captured from B2B employers.
